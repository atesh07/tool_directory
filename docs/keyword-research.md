# Keyword research (Google Autocomplete, 22 Oct 2026)

**Method.** For each tool I typed the obvious search into Google's autocomplete and kept what Google offers. Autocomplete shows what many people really type, so it is a good guide to wording. It does **not** give search volume or competition. Get those numbers from Search Console (your own data), Google Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account) or a paid tool, and re-check every few months.

## What this tells us

- **Competition is strong** on the plain phrases: the suggestions are full of iLovePDF, Smallpdf, Sejda and 11zon. A new site will not win "merge pdf" soon. Chances are better on narrower phrases.
- **Our real edge:** "same font" (an actual suggestion, and what Edit PDF does), "no upload / without login", and Hinglish how-to searches, where there is much less good content.
- **Demand we cannot serve yet:** compress to 100/200/300 KB, PDF to Word, protect/unlock. Build those tools before writing pages for them. Do not claim them.

## What to do with Search Console

Search Console has your real numbers. Look at it every week or two (Performance > Search results, last 28 days):

1. **Pages with many impressions but a low click rate** (under about 2%, when your position is better than 10): rewrite the title and description so they answer the search more directly.
2. **Queries at position 8 to 20**: these are close. Add a section or FAQ answering exactly that query on the page, and link to the page from related guides.
3. **Queries with no matching page**: if several people search for something you do not cover, write a short guide for it (only if you can say something useful).
4. **Pages with no impressions after 6 to 8 weeks**: check they are indexed (URL Inspection), then improve or merge them with a stronger page.
5. Change one thing at a time and write down the date, so you can see what helped.
